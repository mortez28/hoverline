Privacy Policy for Kestrel IPTV Player

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Kestrel IPTV Player ("the app") is a media player published by Hoverline Apps. It does not include, sell or provide any TV channels, movies, series or other content. You add your own playlist, and the app plays what that playlist contains.

What the app stores

  • Playlist details you enter: the server address, username and password, or the playlist link. These are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and are sent only to the server you entered so it can return your playlist and streams.
  • Your preferences: favorites, watch progress, audio and subtitle choices, and your parental control PIN. These are stored only on your device.

We do not have a server that receives this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information, contacts, location or device identifiers.
  • It does not include advertising or analytics.
  • It does not share any data with us or with third parties.

Network connections

The app connects to:

  • The servers in your playlist, to load channel lists, programme guides, images and video streams. Those servers are run by whoever provides your playlist, not by us, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • Google Play, to process the optional Premium purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Cast (Premium), when you choose to play on a Chromecast or other Cast device on your network.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It includes an optional PIN to lock categories and channels.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
