Privacy Policy for Songbird Prompter

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Songbird Prompter (“the app”, listed on Google Play as “Songbird: Teleprompter & Video”) is published by Hoverline Apps.

What stays on your device

  • Camera and microphone: used to show the camera behind your script and to record when you press Record. Videos are saved to your phone’s gallery and are not uploaded.
  • Background blur (optional): to tell you apart from the background, each camera frame is checked by a person-detection model (Google ML Kit selfie segmentation) that is built into the app and runs on your phone, also offline. No frames or results are uploaded or stored.
  • Scripts and settings: stored in the app on your phone. If Android backup is on, Android may include them in your Google account backup.
  • On-phone captions: the “Standard” and “More accurate” caption options turn speech into text on your phone. A speech model is downloaded once; your audio is not uploaded.
  • Script import: files, pasted text and photos you import are read on your phone. Photos of text are read on your phone by Google’s ML Kit unless you choose “Read with AI” (see below). A web link you import is downloaded by the app directly from that site, or from Google Docs, Drive or Dropbox for document links, not through our server.
  • Phone remote: when you turn it on, the app runs a small web page on your phone that only devices on the same local network (your Wi‑Fi or the phone’s hotspot) can open, and only with the secret link in the QR code. The remote phone sees the prompter’s current line, timer and speed and sends button presses. Nothing goes through our server or the internet, and it stops when you leave the prompter.
  • Floating prompter: shows your script over other apps. It only draws its own window and can’t see, read or record the other app.

Features that use the internet (optional)

  • Online captions: when you choose “Online (best)”, the app sends the recording’s sound (compressed, without the picture) and the script you chose to match against to our server. The server uses Google’s Gemini model on Google Cloud (Vertex AI) to write the captions and sends them back. The sound and text are not stored after the request.
  • AI script help: the text you ask about, and any topic you type or speak, is sent to our server and to Google’s Gemini model to write the suggestion. “Make it a script with AI” sends imported text the same way, and “Read with AI” sends a photo of text (shrunk) to copy out its words. Nothing is stored after the request.
  • Voice pacing and voice input: use your phone’s speech recognizer. Where the phone has an offline model for your language, speech stays on the device; otherwise the phone’s speech service (usually Google’s) processes it under that provider’s privacy terms. The app keeps no audio or transcript from voice pacing.

Usage counts

To apply the free and monthly limits for online captions and AI help, the app signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. Our server stores a random user ID with how many requests it has used and when. No name, email or phone number is linked to it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data starts a new ID. To have usage counts deleted, email us.

Purchases

Premium is a subscription handled by Google Play. We see the purchase status from Google Play, never your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don’t do

  • No ads and no analytics.
  • We don’t sell or share personal data. We use Google Cloud (Firebase and Vertex AI) only to provide the online features above.
  • All data sent to our server is encrypted in transit.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
