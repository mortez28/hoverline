Songbird Prompter

A teleprompter for Android. Read your script over the camera, record in one take, and add captions on your phone.

On Google Play as “Songbird: Teleprompter & Video”. Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic and Persian.

On your phone

Recording and captions, illustrated with a made-up script and person.

Prompter and recording

  • Script scrolls over the live camera, front or back
  • Speed, text size, width, background, mirror and flip
  • Near-lens mode: a few lines right under the front camera, so you look at your viewers
  • Rehearse without recording, then record and scroll together
  • Countdown with beeps, auto-stop at the end of the script
  • Zoom, flash, exposure, focus lock, grid, sound meter and mic choice
  • 720p and 1080p, or 4K on phones that support it (Premium); stabilization
  • Background blur: you stay sharp and the room behind you is softened, live and in the recording, worked out on your phone (Premium)
  • Dual camera: back camera with a front-camera window, on supported phones
  • Videos save to your gallery; watch, rename, share or delete several at once
  • If the screen locks or a call comes in mid-take, the take is kept up to that moment
  • Right-to-left scripts (Persian, Arabic)

Remotes

  • Use a second phone as the remote: scan a QR code and its browser becomes the remote, no app needed
  • Play, record, speed, scroll and a drag pad, with the current line and timer on the remote
  • Bluetooth remotes, page turners, foot pedals, keyboards and game controllers
  • Set any button to any action in Settings

Scripts

  • Import from Word, PDF (scanned too), Google Docs, Drive, Dropbox, web links, RTF, ODT, EPUB, Markdown and subtitles
  • Share to Songbird from any app, or photograph a printed page
  • Headings, emphasis, highlights and cues like [pause] show on the prompter
  • Tap a mark to remove it, autosave, folders, pins, search and undo
  • A sample script to try the prompter the moment you open the app
  • Export to Word, share as text, or back up every script in one file

Scenes and editing

  • Record scene by scene and retake only the part you got wrong
  • Pick the best take of each scene, reorder, and join them into one video
  • Trim long pauses automatically, and cut filler words like “um” (Premium)

Floating prompter

  • Your script in a small window over Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Zoom or any camera app
  • Resize, move, adjust opacity, control it from the notification or a remote
  • It only draws the window; it can’t see or record the other app

Auto captions

  • Make captions on the phone, or online for the best accuracy (great for Persian and Arabic)
  • Matches captions to your script for correct spelling, with word-by-word timing
  • Edit text, pick a style and position, check them full screen over the video
  • Burn them into a new video, or save an .srt file
  • Keeps working with the screen off

AI script help and voice pacing

  • Write a script from a topic, get a stronger opening, tighten, shorten or change the tone
  • Turn imported notes into a script, or read handwriting and Persian or Arabic photos with AI
  • Voice pacing: the script follows your voice as you speak (Premium)

Free and Premium

  • Script import, export, marks and backup
  • Scripts up to 1,000 words
  • Recordings with a small “Made with Songbird Prompter” mark
  • Videos of up to 3 scenes
  • Trim pauses; 720p and 1080p
  • Timed scrolling
  • Captions in the Clean style at the bottom
  • 3 videos of online captions and 5 AI requests to try
  • 3 floating prompter and 3 phone remote sessions to try

Premium, monthly or yearly

  • Voice-paced scrolling
  • No watermark on recordings, scene videos and caption videos
  • Unlimited scenes per video
  • Background blur; cut filler words; 4K recording
  • All caption styles (Bold, Karaoke word highlight, Subtitle bar) and positions
  • Online captions for 100 videos a month
  • AI script help: 300 requests a month
  • Unlimited floating prompter and phone remote
  • Scripts of any length

Manage or cancel any time in Google Play.

Private by design

Scripts and videos stay on your phone, and background blur runs on the phone too. Only the online features you choose send anything to our server, and nothing is kept after the request. No ads, no analytics. Read the privacy policy and terms of service.
